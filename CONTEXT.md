# Business Context — Creator Partnership Model

## What This Business Is
I run a digital product partnership service for social media creators.
I reach out to creators with 10k–250k followers who have not yet
monetised their audience. I build them a digital product, handle all
the technical setup, and we split revenue automatically via Whop.

## The Deal Structure
- Revenue split: 70% to the creator, 30% to me
- I handle: product creation, Whop checkout setup,
  sales page, welcome page, creator landing page,
  user guide and content templates for promotion
- They handle: posting about the product to their audience
- Payment is split automatically through Whop

## Current Niche Focus
- PRIMARY NICHE: ADHD creators on Instagram
- Target: Male creators only for now
- Target follower range: 15k–100k
- Ideal creator: Male, shares personal ADHD experiences,
  has engaged comments, no existing products in bio,
  has email in bio OR open DMs
- Sub-niches: ADHD coaching, late diagnosed ADHD,
  ADHD and work, ADHD and fitness, ADHD entrepreneurship,
  ADHD and relationships, ADHD and finance

## Target Customer (The Creator's Audience)
- People with ADHD looking for systems, tools and structure
- Pain points: focus, routine, overwhelm, procrastination,
  emotional dysregulation, rejection sensitivity
- High buying intent — impulsive buyers who trust creators they follow

## Outreach Strategy
- Primary channel: Email only
- Lead source: Manus finds male creators with
  public emails on Instagram
- Email tool: Built in Claude Code
- Daily send limit: 20 emails maximum
- Follow up 1: 5 days after initial email
- Follow up 2: 5 days after follow up 1
- All outreach logged to leads spreadsheet

## The Outreach Sequence
### Email Subject Line
Curiosity based — specific to their content

### Initial Email
Hey [Name],

I've been going through your content and honestly
the way you [SPECIFIC OBSERVATION] is something
most ADHD accounts completely miss.

Your audience isn't just watching you for entertainment
— they're watching you because they trust you.

Have you ever thought about turning that into something
they could actually use every single day?

I build digital products for ADHD creators — fully
done for you, zero effort on your end — and you keep
70% of every sale automatically.

I've put together a quick one-pager showing exactly
what this could look like for your audience specifically
— would you be open to taking a look?

[Your name]

### After They Reply With Interest
Ask: "Can I ask — is there one thing your followers
message you about more than anything else?"

### After They Answer
Validate their answer + hint at product angle, then:
"I've put together a quick one-pager showing how this
could work — want me to send it over?"

### After They Say Yes
Send the blueprint. End with offer to make a
personalised version to keep momentum going.

### Follow Up 1 (5 days after no reply)
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hey [Name]!

Just wanted to bump this in case it got buried —
genuinely think your audience would love something
like this 😊

[Your name]

### Follow Up 2 (5 days after Follow Up 1)
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Last nudge I promise 😄 — if the timing isn't right
no worries at all, just didn't want you to miss it
if you were curious!

[Your name]

### Ghosting Recovery (4 days after mid-conversation ghost)
Hey! No pressure at all — just wanted to check if
you had any questions about how it works?
Happy to keep it super casual 😊

### Re-approach (for leads gone cold 2+ months)
Hey! Sorry for dropping off — life got busy 😄
I still think what I had in mind for your audience
could be really valuable. Would you be open to
a quick chat about it?
